# NOIOP Pilot Execution Master Plan v1.0

Platform: National Opportunity Intelligence & Orchestration Platform (NOIOP)
Release track: Controlled Institutional Pilot
Status: PILOT EXECUTION BASELINE ESTABLISHED

## 1. Purpose

This plan governs the transition from the accepted public-safe Operational MVP into a controlled institutional pilot. The pilot is designed to generate reproducible operational evidence without exposing protected trade-secret implementation details.

## 2. Pilot Objectives

1. Validate end-to-end opportunity assessment and orchestration workflows in a controlled institutional context.
2. Measure decision-support usefulness, consistency, traceability and operating latency.
3. Validate human authority boundaries and escalation controls.
4. Establish evidence that outputs can be reviewed, challenged and traced to their operational inputs.
5. Measure integration readiness without requiring unrestricted production access.
6. Produce a formal evidence package suitable for pilot acceptance and subsequent acquisition diligence.

## 3. Controlled Pilot Scope

The pilot may use approved synthetic, anonymized, sandbox or expressly authorized institutional data. Production credentials, unrestricted production systems and confidential third-party information are outside scope unless separately authorized.

Pilot workflow:

Intake -> Validation -> Assessment -> Prioritization -> Orchestration Recommendation -> Human Review -> Decision -> Evidence Capture -> Outcome Evaluation.

## 4. Success Gates

Gate P1: Environment and access readiness.
Gate P2: Data-quality acceptance.
Gate P3: Functional scenario execution.
Gate P4: Human-authority and governance validation.
Gate P5: Performance and reliability validation.
Gate P6: Evidence completeness.
Gate P7: Institutional acceptance decision.

A pilot cannot be represented as accepted until all mandatory gates have documented evidence and an authorized acceptance decision.

## 5. Evidence Rule

Every material pilot claim must map to a reproducible evidence artifact. Unsupported assertions are not acceptance evidence.

## 6. Intellectual Property Boundary

The pilot grants no ownership, assignment, license beyond expressly agreed pilot-use rights, waiver, transfer or public-domain dedication. Protected implementation details remain controlled and should be disclosed only under the applicable confidentiality and due-diligence controls.

## 7. Exit States

PASS: mandatory gates satisfied.
CONDITIONAL PASS: defined remediation remains but does not invalidate the controlled pilot objective.
FAIL: one or more mandatory acceptance conditions are not met.
NOT TESTED: evidence is insufficient to make a determination.

## 8. Next Release Condition

Production readiness is a separate maturity gate. Successful pilot completion alone does not constitute production certification.
